**Key Takeaways**
1. Balance Curiosity with Value Creation
	1. Explore new AI capabilities but always anchor experiments to real business outcomes.
2. Start with the Problem, Not the Technology
	1. Identify the questions your business needs to answer first, then work backwards to find the right solution.
3. Know Your Data First
	1. Understand your data sources before selecting your tools.
4. Match the Tool to the Data
	•	Unstructured data (PDFs, documents) → Cortex Search
	•	Extract structured data from documents → Document AI
	•	Structured data (tables, databases) → Cortex Analyst
	•	Both → Cortex Agent to orchestrate​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​
