Query Your Life

Ask anything. In plain English.

Your notes become a personal database. Ask questions and get instant, data-backed answers.

You ask
"How many times did I squat over 200 lbs this month?"
Answer
You squatted over 200 lbs on 6 days this month, with your best session at 225 lbs x 8 reps.
You ask
"What's my average sleep when I train legs?"
Answer
On leg days you average 7.2 hours of sleep, vs 6.8h on other days. Your mood is also 0.5 points higher.
You ask
"Show me what I ate this week"
Answer
Mon: Oatmeal, chicken salad, pasta · ~2,100 cal
Tue: Eggs, salmon bowl, stir fry · ~2,350 cal
Wed: Shake, burrito, soup · ~1,900 cal
Avg: 2,117 cal/day · Protein: 142g avg
You ask
"On days I sleep 8+ hours, how's my energy?"
Answer
When you sleep 8+ hours, your energy averages 8.4/10 vs 6.7/10 otherwise. That's a 25% boost.

Database

Two engines. One interface.

Choose the right storage for your setup. Switch between them without changing your code.

🦆

DuckDB

Local Mode
  • ✓ Zero configuration — just a file
  • ✓ Blazing fast analytical queries
  • ✓ Perfect for single-user setups
  • ✓ No external database needed
  • ✓ Portable — copy one file to back up
or
🐘

Postgres

Cloud Mode
  • ✓ Production-ready scaling
  • ✓ Multi-user with Clerk auth
  • ✓ Connect to any Postgres instance
  • ✓ DuckDB analytics cache layer
  • ✓ Remote access via Tailscale
