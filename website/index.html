How It Works

Three steps. Zero forms.

No dropdowns, no spreadsheets, no rigid input fields. Just write like you normally would.

01

Write naturally

Open your daily note and write. No structure required. Just describe your day, your workout, your meals, your thoughts.

Trained legs today.
Squats 3x8 at 225.
Felt great, energy was high.
Sleep was 7.5 hours.
02

AI extracts the data

Claude reads your notes and pulls out structured data points. Workouts, meals, sleep, mood, tasks — all automatically.

exercise: squats
weight: 225 lbs
sets: 3 · reps: 8
sleep: 7.5 hours
03

Query your life

Ask any question in plain English. Your data becomes a personal analytics engine. Charts, trends, insights — on demand.

"How much did I squat
last month?"

████ Wk1: 205 lbs
█████ Wk2: 215 lbs
██████ Wk3: 225 lbs
Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

Built for people who think in prose, not spreadsheets.

Rich markdown editor

A beautiful writing experience powered by Milkdown. Templates, WYSIWYG toolbar, and autosave. Write naturally — the editor gets out of your way.

Milkdown-powered

Smart extraction

Claude AI reads your notes and extracts workouts, meals, sleep, moods, and tasks into structured data. No tagging, no manual entry.

Powered by Claude

Visual dashboard

Charts, heatmaps, and trends. See your life data at a glance. Track progress over weeks, months, and years without lifting a finger.

Your data, your control

Your notes live in YOUR Google Drive. Your extracted data is isolated in your own database. Export everything, anytime. No vendor lock-in.
