How It Works

Three steps. Zero forms.

Write like you would in Apple Notes or a journal. No dropdowns, no spreadsheets. The AI handles the structure.

01

Write naturally

Open your daily note and write. No structure required. Just describe your day, your workout, your meals, your thoughts.

Trained legs today.
Squats 3x8 at 225.
Felt great, energy was high.
Sleep was 7.5 hours.
02

AI extracts the data

Claude reads your notes and pulls out structured data points. Workouts, meals, sleep, mood, tasks — all automatically.

exercise: squats
weight: 225 lbs
sets: 3 · reps: 8
sleep: 7.5 hours
03

Query your life

Ask any question in plain English. Your data becomes a personal analytics engine. Charts, trends, insights — on demand.

"How much did I squat
last month?"

████ Wk1: 205 lbs
█████ Wk2: 215 lbs
██████ Wk3: 225 lbs
Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

Built for people who think in prose, not spreadsheets.

Rich markdown editor

A beautiful writing experience powered by Milkdown. Templates, WYSIWYG toolbar, table editing, and autosave. Write naturally — the editor gets out of your way.

Milkdown-powered

Smart extraction

Claude AI reads your notes and extracts workouts, meals, sleep, moods, and tasks into structured data. AI-powered exercise normalization matches variations to canonical names automatically.

Powered by Claude

Visual dashboard

Activity heatmaps, nutrition tracking with calories and macros, mood correlations against sleep and exercise, and weekly charts with stacked exercise categories. Your life at a glance.

Daily note wizard + task rollover

Create structured daily notes in seconds. The wizard prompts you through goals, metrics, and priorities — and automatically surfaces incomplete tasks from previous days so nothing falls through the cracks.

New in v0.8

Personal kanban

Drag-and-drop task board with two-way markdown sync. Check off a task in your note and it moves on the board. Move it on the board and the note updates. Deadlines, categories, and task detail modals included.

Self-host or cloud

Run locally with DuckDB for full privacy, or deploy to the cloud with Postgres and Clerk auth for multi-user access. Same app, two modes — your choice. No vendor lock-in, ever.

Docker ready
